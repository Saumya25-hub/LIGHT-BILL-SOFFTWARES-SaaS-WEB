## 🏭 Major Features in v2.2.7

### 1. Weaving Mill & Karkhana Factory Floor Engine
- **Live Loom Floor Monitoring**: Real-time status grid for Waterjet, Airjet, Rapier, and Sulzer looms with instant indicators (Running, Idle, Stop, Maintenance).
- **Warp Beam Register & Run-Out Math**: Auto-calculates Total Ends, Reed Count, and forecasts beam run-out meters in real time.
- **Taka Production Register & Roll Cutter**: 1-click taka cut with auto-incrementing Lot No & Taka No, actual meters, weight in Kg, and average grams/meter (g/m) density.
- **Floor-to-Sales 1-Click Taka Picker**: Seamlessly select In-Stock rolls inside Sales Invoices. Ticked rolls auto-populate billable meters and lot numbers without re-typing and transition to Sold status on bill save.
- **Multilingual Karkhana Floor Guide**: Step-by-step interactive onboarding guide in Hinglish, Gujarati, and English with an isolated Practice Demo Sandbox.

### 2. Selective Multi-Company Sync Pool
- **Opt-In Sync Pool Selection**: Check only the companies that should share catalog products and parties (e.g. 3 textile sister concerns), while keeping retail shops or personal enterprises 100% private, clean, and unflooded.
- **Strict Data Isolation Guarantee**: Stock quantities, invoices, financial ledgers, and bank balances remain 100% air-gapped per company SQLite database.
- **Zero-Flood New Company Creation**: Newly created companies start completely clean and isolated by default.

### 3. Statutory BOS E-Way Bill & Dispatch Engine
- Full statutory Rule 138 compliance: 1-click NIC JSON export, embedded portal automation with WebView2, cryptographic SHA-256 PDF vault verification, and multi-invoice batch dispatch.

### 4. High-Speed Counter Billing & Thermal Print
- Ultra-fast 0ms offline SQLite transactions with keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+P party picker, Ctrl+N new creation, Ctrl+E inline edit).
- Native 80mm & 58mm ESC/POS thermal receipt printing with auto-cutter support.
